=============================================================
Account Invoice: Partner-specific income and expense accounts
=============================================================

* Allow defining income and expense accounts for partner

Configuration
=============
* In partner form, in Invoicing tab, set the new Income Account and
  Expense Account fields. Note that these are company-speficic properties
  in multicompany environments

Usage
=====
* Create an invoice or a refund for that partner. Invoice lines' accounts
  get suggested according to the filled in partner accounts
